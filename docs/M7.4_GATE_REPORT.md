### Caveats

1. **Two `bright_frb_extragalactic` triggers fired in the first ~60 s** of the
   soak. Cause: the on-disk `c2_trigger_criteria.yaml` was edited in place to
   flip `dump_all_gpus` → `log_only` *while* the C2 service was already up; the
   service's mtime poll only fires on each batch processing, so 2 batches
   slipped through with the pre-flip criteria. **The 2 events are valid
   extragalactic detections** (dm_median ≈ 2 400 pc/cc vs gal_dm ≈ 35 pc/cc;
   `dm_galactic_fraction` ≈ 69×, comfortably above the 0.75 cutoff), but
   they triggered real `dump_all_gpus` actions on the search nodes. For
   *strict* future gate runs, restart C2 with `--criteria <path/to/log_only.yaml>`
   instead of in-place editing.
2. **n13 carries an outsized candidate count** (~14k cands vs ~1.5k for the
   other 3 nodes combined). This is a known chronic n13 quirk (likely a hot
   pixel or RFI residue specific to the chgroup mapping that lands on the
   n13 halves). Cubes/s is still at gate target on both n13 halves, and the
   excess cands are absorbed correctly by the C2 components clusterer
   (graph_size = 391, no runaway). Not a gate-blocker; flag for Phase 6 root
   cause (likely a per-pixel cal blob issue).

### What the gate proves

- The 16×4 production pipeline at the full production op-point hits real-time
  cube cadence (≥7.45/s) on every search-node half, with zero drops over 30
  minutes of continuous real SNAP traffic.
- The full C1/C2 stack (detector → merger → cluster log → coincidencer →
  classifier → action) executes cleanly end-to-end.
- The Galactic-DM C2 discriminant (Phase 5 code) is correctly wired end-to-end:
  poller against `/mon/array/gal_dm` is healthy, `dm_galactic_fraction` is
  computed per cluster, and the extragalactic branch fires on candidates that
  satisfy the 0.75-cutoff (validated by the 2 pre-reload triggers).
- RFI excision (SK + bandpass + group + sumthr + 5-σ flag-ant) is active on
  the corr side at ~8 % flag fraction, with no impact on RT cadence.

### What the gate does **not** prove

- The `bright_galactic` C2 branch (Phase 5 Galactic side) is exercised only
  in unit tests — no real low-DM Galactic burst landed in the 30-min window.
  An injection or a Crab-coincident run will close that.
- DM-plan rebuild path: any change to the subband layout invalidates the v2
  plan and would force a rebuild (we're using a fixed v2 plan).
- n13 over-cand source.
