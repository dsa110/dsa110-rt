Array sums — core and arm autocorrelations

Every detector in the deployed flagger runs inside one antenna, and each needs a reference that the signal does not also move. Bandpass-outlier compares a channel against the other channels of the same antenna; group-outlier compares an antenna against the other antennas; spectral kurtosis asks whether the voltages are Gaussian within an accumulation. A broadband burst common to the whole array defeats all three by construction — measured on 260812imek sb02, such a burst is 0.05 σ in the cell the flagger tests and 13.3 σ in the array-summed band power at 2.097 ms. This page shows that sum, treating the core and each of its arms as extra antennas.

Band-summed significance at 2.097 ms — chgroup {{ view.chgroup }}

One point per 2.097 ms accumulation — the finest the flagger already forms (autos.s1[64], built for spectral kurtosis), so the sum costs no new moments. Red verticals mark samples the flag group fired on. Each antenna is divided by its own running per-channel mean before summing, and dead antenna-pols are excluded outright: leaving them in put the all-96 null at 0.978 ± 0.026 instead of 1.000 ± 0.0013.

Arm symmetry — chgroup {{ view.chgroup }}

A far-field source illuminates both arms in proportion to their collecting area, so it lands on the 1:1 line. A local source does not — the 260812imek bursts sit at 1.86% E-W against 0.81% N-S, a 2.3× asymmetry. This is a diagnostic only: the firing decision uses band-summed significance and broadband occupancy, not the arm ratio.
